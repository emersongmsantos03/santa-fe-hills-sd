Buying in Santa Fe Hills? 5 things to check before you make an offer

By Lara Gabriele, REALTOR® · November 2026

Santa Fe Hills is one of those San Marcos neighborhoods people go looking for on purpose. Homes don't come up often, so when one does, you want to be ready to move. Here's what to check first.

1. HOA: none, or a small one?

Many streets in Santa Fe Hills have no HOA at all, which is a big part of the appeal. But some pockets, like Rancho Santalina and some newer builds, do have their own associations with monthly dues. Don't assume. Ask for the HOA status of the exact address, and if there is one, read the CC&Rs and the budget before you're out of contingencies.

2. Mello-Roos and special taxes

Listings here often mention low Mello-Roos, but special assessments are set property by property. Pull the actual tax bill for the home you're considering so you know your real monthly cost, not an estimate.

3. Solar: owned, leased or financed?

Plenty of homes in the area have solar. Paid-off panels are a plus. A lease or a loan can come with a transfer process and a monthly payment you'll take over. Get the contract early.

4. School assignments

Santa Fe Hills is in San Marcos Unified School District. Boundaries can change, so confirm the assigned schools for the specific address directly with the district.

5. The age of big-ticket items

Most homes here were built between 1990 and 2005. That means roofs, water heaters, HVAC systems and windows may be original or recently replaced, and the difference matters. A good inspection, and asking the seller for receipts, will tell you which one you're getting.

The bottom line

With few homes for sale at any time, the buyers who win in Santa Fe Hills are the ones who did this homework before the right house appeared. Get pre-approved, set up alerts, and know the neighborhood street by street.
