About Santa Fe Hills

A neighborhood people don't leave easily

Santa Fe Hills is a hillside neighborhood in San Marcos, developed mostly between 1990 and the mid-2000s. You'll find well-kept single-family homes, quiet cul-de-sacs and views across the hills, with homes from roughly 1,050 to 3,300 square feet.

Buyers love it for what it doesn't have: many streets carry no HOA, and listings regularly point to low Mello-Roos. Some pockets do have their own small associations, so it pays to check street by street.

Because owners tend to stay, homes here don't come up often. When they do, the right preparation and pricing make all the difference.

Living here

Why people choose Santa Fe Hills

Trails & open space

Hillside trail access nearby, with Double Peak Park and Discovery Lake a short drive away for sunrise hikes and lake loops.

Campus town energy

Cal State San Marcos and Palomar College are close, along with the walkable North City district.

Everyday convenience

Costco, Kaiser, Palomar Medical Center and the Carlsbad business parks are all minutes away.

Beach days

Take SR-78 west and you're at the Carlsbad coast in about 20 minutes.

Santa Fe Hills FAQ

Does Santa Fe Hills have an HOA?

Many streets in Santa Fe Hills have no HOA at all. Some pockets, like Rancho Santalina and some newer builds, have their own associations with monthly dues. Lara can tell you exactly what applies to a specific address.

What schools serve Santa Fe Hills?

Santa Fe Hills is served by San Marcos Unified School District. Boundaries can change, so always confirm the assigned schools for a specific address with the district.

How much do homes in Santa Fe Hills cost?

Recent resales have mostly landed between about $900K and $1.3M, depending on size, condition, lot and views. Newer construction can sell above that. Ask Lara for a current report.

Are there Mello-Roos taxes in Santa Fe Hills?

Listings in Santa Fe Hills often mention low Mello-Roos, but special taxes vary by property. Check the tax bill for the home you're considering, and Lara can help you pull it.

How quickly do Santa Fe Hills homes sell?

Homes here don't come up often, and well-prepared, well-priced homes tend to move quickly. If you're thinking of selling, a pricing plan based on the latest nearby sales is the best place to start.
